How to play

Woordle is a mini crossword Wordle. Every puzzle has three words: a 5-letter word across, a 4-letter word down, and a 3-letter word down, and all three fit a theme — like Farm, Space, or Ocean — shown above the board as a clue.

The two down words each cross the across word at one shared letter. Crossing spots are random, and those two shared letters are already filled in to get you started. A letter you type in a crossing cell counts for both words.

Fill in the entire crossword, then press Enter. That uses 1 of your 7 tries. You submit the whole puzzle at once, not one word at a time.

A Green — right letter, right spot.
A Yellow — in that word, but in a different spot.
A Blue — not in this word, but it is in one of the other two words.
A Gray — not in any of the three words.

Yellow is only about the word that letter sits in. Blue means the letter belongs somewhere else in the puzzle. Green letters stay locked for the next try.

Tap a cell to type there. On a crossing, tap again to switch between across and down. There's a new themed Daily every day, the same for everyone in the same timezone. You get one extra free hint each day. Practice anytime, and pick any theme you like.
