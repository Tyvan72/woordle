How to play

Woordle is a mini crossword Wordle. Every puzzle has three words: a 5-letter word across, a 4-letter word down, and a 3-letter word down, and all three fit a theme — like Farm, Space, or Ocean — shown above the board as a clue.

The two down words each cross the across word at one shared letter. Crossing spots are random, and a letter you type in a crossing cell counts for both words.

Fill in the entire crossword, then press Enter. That uses 1 of your 7 tries. You submit the whole puzzle at once, not one word at a time.

A Green — right letter, right spot.
A Yellow — in that word, but in a different spot.
A Gray — not in that word.

Yellow is only about the word that letter sits in, not the other two. Green letters stay locked for the next try.

Tap a cell to type there. On a crossing, tap again to switch between across and down. There's a new themed Daily every day, the same for everyone in the same timezone. Practice anytime, and pick any theme you like.
