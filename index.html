How to play

Woordle is a mini crossword Wordle. Every puzzle has three words: a 5-letter word across, a 4-letter word down, and a 3-letter word down.

The two down words each cross the across word at one shared letter. Crossing spots are random — they can be any letter, not just the ends or the middle.

You have 7 guesses for the whole puzzle, not per word. Every try on any word uses one of those 7.

A Green — right letter, right spot.
A Yellow — in that word, but in a different spot.
A Gray — not in that word.

Yellow only counts for the word you're guessing, not the other two.

When a letter turns green, that crossword cell fills in for the crossing word too — a free clue.

Tap a word (or its tiles) to guess it. There's a new Daily puzzle every day, the same for everyone in the same timezone. Practice anytime.
